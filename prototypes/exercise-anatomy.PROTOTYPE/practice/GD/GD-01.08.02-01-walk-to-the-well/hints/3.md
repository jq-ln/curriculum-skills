# Hint 3

Inside `_physics_process(delta)`:

1. Set only the horizontal parts of `velocity`, `x` and `z`, from `heading() * speed`, so the vertical part keeps falling.
2. Add `get_gravity() * delta` to `velocity`. Gravity is an acceleration, so this one does take `delta`.
3. Call `move_and_slide()`. On the floor it cancels the downward part for you, and against the steep slope it removes the part going into it.

Nothing else is needed: the 30-degree ramp counts as floor and the 60-degree slope as wall because of `floor_max_angle`, 45 degrees by default.
