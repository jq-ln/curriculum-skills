# Hint 2

Each physics tick, set the body's `velocity` from `heading()` and `speed`, add the gravity `get_gravity()` reports for one tick, then call `move_and_slide()`. Don't multiply the walking velocity by `delta`: `move_and_slide()` already moves the body by `velocity` over one step.

If the villager walks but never falls, or falls but never walks, look at which parts of `velocity` you overwrite each tick.
