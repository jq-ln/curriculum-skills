## Goal

An ordered pair tells its first element from its second, so (a, b) and (b, a) differ unless a equals b.

## Scope

Ordered pairs and when two of them are equal. Stops before tuples of any length.

## Key concepts

ordered pair; equality

## Check

When are (a, b) and (b, a) the same pair?
